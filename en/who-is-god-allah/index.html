Who is God (Allah)?

"Allah" is simply the Arabic word for God — the same word used by Arab Christians. He is One, without partner, and nothing is like Him.

“Say, He is Allāh, [who is] One, Allāh, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, Nor is there to Him any equivalent.”

Quran 112:1-4

The verses are quoted word for word from an official translation of the Quran: Saheeh International (Noor International Center) — QuranEnc.com ↗
