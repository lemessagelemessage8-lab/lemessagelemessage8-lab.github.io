What is the message of Islam?

"Islam" means submission to the One God, and comes from the same root as "salam": peace. Muslims believe that God sent prophets to every people — Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and others — and that Muhammad ﷺ is the last of them, sent with the Quran for all of humanity.

وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةً لِّلْعَالَمِينَ

“And We have not sent you, [O Muḥammad], except as a mercy to the worlds.”

Quran 21:107 — The Prophets

The five pillars of Islam

  1. L'attestation de foi (Chahada) : il n'y a de dieu que Dieu, et Muhammad est Son messager
  2. La prière (Salat), cinq fois par jour
  3. L'aumône obligatoire (Zakat) pour les plus démunis
  4. Le jeûne du mois de Ramadan (Sawm)
  5. Le pèlerinage à La Mecque (Hajj), pour qui en a les moyens

The six pillars of faith

  1. Croire en Dieu, l'Unique
  2. Croire en Ses anges
  3. Croire en Ses livres révélés (Torah, Psaumes, Évangile, Coran)
  4. Croire en Ses messagers
  5. Croire au Jour dernier
  6. Croire au destin

The film “The Message” (1976)

Moustapha Akkad's film tells the story of the birth of Islam and of the message brought by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Out of respect, the Prophet is never shown or heard in it. The English version stars Anthony Quinn and Irene Papas; the film was restored in 4K in 2018, and this site takes its name from it.

Themes of the message

Short introductions with verses of the Quran. Each theme can be shared with a friend.

Why were we created?

The Quran answers one of humanity's oldest questions: life has a meaning — to know and worship our Creator, and to do good on earth.

“And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me.”Quran 51:56

Read more →

Who is God (Allah)?

"Allah" is simply the Arabic word for God — the same word used by Arab Christians. He is One, without partner, and nothing is like Him.

“Say, He is Allāh, [who is] One, Allāh, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, Nor is there to Him any equivalent.”Quran 112:1-4

Read more →
📖

What is the Quran?

The Quran is the word of God revealed to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over 23 years, preserved in Arabic to this day. It is a guide for all people.

“This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allāh”Quran 2:2

Read more →
☾

Who is Muhammad ﷺ?

Born in Mecca around 570, he was known as "al-Amin", the trustworthy.

“And indeed, you are of a great moral character.”Quran 68:4

Read more →
❤

Mercy

Each chapter of the Quran but one begins with "In the name of Allāh, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful". Mercy towards people, animals and the earth is at the heart of the message.

“… My mercy encompasses all things…”Quran 7:156

Read more →
🌍

One humanity

No people is superior to another by race or origin. Diversity is a sign of God, and the best of people is the most righteous.

“… made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allāh is the most righteous of you…”Quran 49:13

Read more →
✿

Women in Islam

Man and woman are equal before God in faith, in deeds and in reward.

“Whoever does righteousness, whether male or female, while he is a believer - We will surely cause him to live a good life…”Quran 16:97

Read more →
🤲

Parents and elders

Kindness to parents comes right after the worship of God.

“And your Lord has decreed that you worship not except Him, and to parents, good treatment…”Quran 17:23

Read more →
🕊

Freedom of belief

Faith cannot be forced: it is a sincere choice of the heart. The role of the Muslim is to convey the message with wisdom and kindness.

“There shall be no compulsion in [acceptance of] the religion…”Quran 2:256

Read more →
