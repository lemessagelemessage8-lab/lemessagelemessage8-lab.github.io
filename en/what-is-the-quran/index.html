What is the Quran?

The Quran is the word of God revealed to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over 23 years, preserved in Arabic to this day. It is a guide for all people.

“This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allāh”

Quran 2:2

The verses are quoted word for word from an official translation of the Quran: Saheeh International (Noor International Center) — QuranEnc.com ↗
